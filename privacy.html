Privacy Policy

DocScan Pro · Updated November 6, 2026

In short: DocScan Pro works without an account, and then your scans stay only on your device. If you choose to create an account, your backed-up scans are kept in the DocScan Pro owner’s Google Drive so you can restore them. We never sell or share your data, show ads or track you.

What the app does with your documents

When you scan or import a document, all processing (finding the page edges, straightening, cleaning and making PDFs) happens on your device. Scans, photos and the documents you create are stored on your device: in your browser’s storage when you use the website, or in the app’s own storage when you use the Android or iPhone app. Unless you create an account and back up (see below), they never leave your device and we cannot see them.

Optional account and backup

Creating an account is optional. If you sign up, we store:

  • your email address, the name you enter, and a scrambled (salted and hashed) version of your password, never the password itself;
  • copies of the scans you back up (the original photo and the cleaned page) and their names, dates and page order.

These are kept in a private Google Drive folder and Google Sheet in the app owner’s Google account, run through Google Apps Script. Each person’s backups are in their own folder, and the app only lets you reach your own. Scans can be medical or other sensitive papers, so only back up what you’re comfortable storing this way. Each account has a storage limit.

You can log out at any time, and Me › Account › Delete account permanently deletes your account and all of your backups. Scans on your device are not affected.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera access only so you can scan documents. Camera images are processed on your device and are not recorded or sent anywhere. If you import photos, only the photos you pick are read, and they stay on your device.

Sharing and saving

When you tap Share or Save, the app hands the file to your phone or computer, which shows its own share menu or file picker. Where the file goes next (for example Gmail, Messenger or Google Drive) is your choice, and that service’s own privacy policy applies to it.

What we do not collect

  • Without an account: no name, email or phone number. With an account: only what is listed above.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising IDs or ads.
  • No location.
  • No copies of your documents unless you turn on backup by creating an account.

Things loaded from the internet

The website loads its font from Google Fonts and its PDF tool from the cdnjs.cloudflare.com code library, and it is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website, those services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when your browser downloads these files. No document content is ever included. The Android and iPhone apps include these files inside the app.

Deleting your data

Delete any document in the app, or remove everything at once under Me › Storage › Delete all documents on this device. To delete backups, use Me › Account › Delete account. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser’s data for this site, also removes them.

Children

The app can be used without giving any personal information. Accounts are optional and are not meant for children under 13; if you believe a child has created one, use Delete account or contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
